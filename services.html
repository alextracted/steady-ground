Service & rates

Diagnose. Maintain. Install. Repair.

We work on the machine where it lives: behind the bar or on your counter. One labor rate covers all of it, plus travel from Blacksburg. Parts are quoted before they're ordered.

Diagnostics

Pressure, flow, temperature stability, and electrical and hydraulic pathways. You get the readings, not just an opinion.

Repair

Group calibrations and rebuilds, screen and gasket replacements, boilers, pumps, valves, grinder burrs, controllers. Common parts ride on the van.

Maintenance

Extend the life of your equipment with inspection and replacement of wear and tear components on an service schedule that suits how hard you run the machine.

Installation

Site survey, plumbing and electrical coordination, setup and dial-in.

Water treatment

Hardness and TDS testing plus filtration solutions optimized for machine longevity and coffee quality.

Home equipment

We fix prosumer espresso machines and grinders produced and supported by reputable specialty coffee brands.

Labor Rate
$100 per hour + travel

Diagnostic, maintenance, install and repair all bill at the same rate. One-hour minimum on every dispatched service call. Travel fees are charged anytime a coffee technician is dispatched to your location for service work.
