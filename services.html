Four ways we keep your coffee running smoothly

Whatever's going on with your machine — broken, overdue for a check-up, brand new, or just confusing — there's a straightforward way we can help.

Something's wrong & you need it fixed

Field Service & Repairs

Leaking, sputtering, suddenly silent, or just not tasting right — a struggling machine usually means one specific part needs attention, not a whole new machine. We come to your home or business, figure out exactly what's going on, and explain it in plain terms before we do anything.

  • On-site diagnosis, so you're not without your machine for weeks
  • Most repairs completed the same visit
  • A clear, honest explanation of what broke and why
  • Covers home and commercial espresso machines & grinders
Keeping small problems small

Routine Maintenance

Most breakdowns don't happen out of nowhere — they build up slowly through mineral scale, worn gaskets, and gunked-up grinder burrs. A regular visit catches all of that early, keeps your coffee tasting the way it's supposed to, and is a lot cheaper than an emergency repair.

  • Descaling, gasket checks, and group head cleaning
  • Grinder burr inspection & calibration
  • Flexible schedules — monthly, quarterly, or seasonal
  • A simple written summary after every visit
Bringing in something new

New Installation Consultation

Buying new equipment is exciting, and a little overwhelming — there are a lot of machines out there, and not all of them fit your space, your water, or your budget. We'll walk through your options with you, then handle the plumbing, electrical, and setup so it's ready to pull a great shot from day one.

  • Honest advice on equipment that fits your needs, not just the priciest option
  • Site visits to check water, power, and space before you buy
  • Full setup, water filtration, and first-run calibration
  • A walkthrough so your team or household knows how to use it
Get hands-on and learn

Skilling Sessions

Booked by the hour, right in your own kitchen or shop, a skilling session is a private coffee lab just for you. Bring your questions, your machine, and your curiosity — we'll dial things in together and you'll walk away actually understanding your setup, not just following steps someone wrote down for you.

  • One-on-one time, paced around your questions
  • Great for beginners and longtime home baristas alike
  • Dial in grind, dose, and technique on your own machine
  • Available for homes and business teams alike

Not sure which one you need?

That's alright — most people aren't. Tell us what's going on and we'll point you in the right direction.
