Coffee's been my whole career. Now it's my neighborhood, too.

The story behind Steady Ground Coffee Service and Skilling, and why it landed in Southwest Virginia.

I'm Alex, and I've spent the last 13 years working in specialty coffee — most of it in Seattle, one of the places that helped shape American coffee culture in the first place. I've supported sales, service, and customer relationships for well-known names in the espresso equipment world, including Slayer Espresso, Visions Espresso Service, Synesso, and Coffee Physics.

Along the way, I became a regular in the home barista community too — the enthusiasts who obsess over their setups at home the same way a shop might. Answering their questions, troubleshooting their machines, and swapping tips turned into one of my favorite parts of the job. Turns out, whether it's a busy café or a kitchen counter, people just want their coffee to be good — and their equipment to stop getting in the way of that.

How Steady Ground got its start

Steady Ground Coffee Service and Skilling LLC came online shortly after my wife and I finished a cross-country move to Blacksburg, Virginia in August 2026. Southwest Virginia has plenty of people who care about good coffee — homes, cafés, and offices with machines that deserve real support — but not a lot of specialists nearby to help take care of them. So I decided to become that resource myself.

"Small, overlooked tweaks are usually all it takes to transform how a machine performs — and how much you enjoy using it every day."

I'm passionate about coffee and a little obsessive about the details. I've owned and maintained several types of coffee and espresso machines and grinders myself, so I know the frustration of a workflow that just isn't working — and how satisfying it is once it finally clicks. Whether you're new to this or you've been pulling shots for twenty years, I'm happy to meet you where you are.

What guides the work

The market for home and commercial coffee equipment keeps changing, and staying useful means staying curious — learning the new machines, the new methods, and the new questions people are asking. My goal is always the same: give you a solution that's personal to your setup, easy to understand, and built to last. That's the Steady Ground bottom line — keeping your equipment at its best, minimizing downtime, and treating it with the care it deserves as the centerpiece of your home or business coffee routine.
